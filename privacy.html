Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Summary

Chen Daily Clips is a private tool for one owner. It runs on the owner's own computer, has no website with accounts, and does not collect personal information from anyone else.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok shares only what the owner approves on TikTok's permission screen: the permission to post videos on the owner's behalf, and basic profile information shown on that screen.

What is stored

  • A login token that lets the app post to the owner's account. It is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file that is readable only by the owner, and is never sent anywhere except to TikTok.
  • The videos, captions and records of what was posted, also stored only on the owner's own computer.

What is shared

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Videos and captions go to TikTok, and to the other platforms the owner posts to, only when the owner publishes them.

Viewer data

The app does not collect, read or store information about people who watch, like or comment on posts.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and websites, and can delete the saved login file on their computer.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
